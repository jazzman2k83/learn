Network and Mode Management in AUTOSAR Classic

Network Management

Above EthSM: when the link comes up

For EthSM, full communication means that IP works. What makes IP work, and what starts once it does, lives in the modules above: TcpIp, the Socket Adaptor, Service Discovery and UdpNm.

The modules above EthSM

On Ethernet, data does not go from PduR straight to the interface. TcpIp implements the protocols of the TCP/IP family (among them IPv4 and IPv6, UDP, TCP, ARP, ICMP and DHCP) and offers sockets. Its upper module is SoAd (Socket Adaptor: maps PDUs to sockets of TcpIp; its socket connections open once TcpIp has an IP address). Above SoAd sit PduR for the I-PDUs of COM, Sd (the Service Discovery module: offers and finds SOME/IP services once the network is up), and UdpNm for the NM PDU.

All of them depend on one thing that EthSM waits for in ETHSM_STATE_WAIT_ONLINE: TcpIp being online, which needs an IP address on the controller.

Service Discovery and the SOME/IP course

Sd starts to offer a service only when two things are there: BswM has set the service as available (a rule of the kind shown in lesson 27), and the IP address is assigned. Then it begins its Initial Wait Phase and sends its first offer. If the address is no longer reported as assigned, Sd enters its Down Phase and the offers stop; when the address is reported assigned again, Sd starts over with the Initial Wait Phase. That happens when TcpIp goes offline, and also during a short link loss: in ONHOLD TcpIp keeps the address but reports it as on hold, and SoAd passes that on to Sd.

The details belong to the SOME/IP in AUTOSAR Classic course: the Sd phases, Sd and BswM and socket connections and routing groups.

UdpNm needs the path too

UdpNm hands every NM PDU to SoAd with SoAd_IfTransmit, and receives through SoAd as well. While the transmission path is not ready, SoAd does not accept the request. This matters after a passive wakeup (a wakeup caused by another ECU): the first NM PDU may come before EthSM has the path open. An option of UdpNm repeats that first transmit request in each main function until SoAd accepts it.

For ComM, nothing of this is visible. The channel Eth reaches full communication with the indication from EthSM in ETHSM_STATE_ONLINE; socket connections, offers and NM PDUs follow from there.

Things to notice. The IP address is the gate: EthSM's ONLINE, SoAd's socket connections and Sd's offers all wait for it. A short link loss (ONHOLD) keeps the address and ComM sees no change, but TcpIp reports the address as on hold, so Sd stops its offers and starts again with the Initial Wait Phase once the link is back.
