Network Management

The EcuM phases

From reset to power off, an ECU passes through five phases. EcuM carries out the fixed sequences at the edges; in between, BswM decides what happens and when.

Five phases

The EcuM of this course is EcuM Flex (the EcuM of AUTOSAR 4.x and later: EcuM runs the startup, sleep and shutdown sequences, while BswM decides when they happen). It divides the life of the ECU into phases. A phase is not a state that the application asks for; it is a stretch of EcuM's own work, or the long stretch in between where EcuM stays in the background.

reset to mode management

STARTUP

The STARTUP phase runs from reset until the OS, the BSW Scheduler and BswM run (lesson 26). It holds only the minimum needed to start mode management.

the long middle

UP

The UP phase is the phase in which the ECU runs. BswM's action lists initialize the rest of the BSW, start the RTE and with it the SWCs, and later stop them again. EcuM stays passive but handles wakeup sources and the alarm clock.

mode management off

SHUTDOWN

The SHUTDOWN phase is the reverse of STARTUP: it shuts down the remaining BSW and the OS and ends in the selected shutdown target (where the ECU goes when it shuts down: off, reset or sleep), here OFF or RESET.

saving energy

SLEEP

In the SLEEP phase the ECU waits for a wakeup with power still on, either halted or polling (lesson 30). A wakeup leads through the WakeupRestart sequence back to UP.

no power

OFF

In the OFF phase the ECU is powered down. It must be startable again, for example by a reset; it can be woken only by wakeup sources with their own power control.

Who decides, who executes

EcuM Flex has no state machine of its own for the running ECU. The specification says it plainly: only BswM can decide when a transition to another mode can be made. EcuM supplies the pieces BswM needs and carries out the sequences.

On the example ECU this means: whether the body gateway may sleep once BodyCan, ChassisCan and Eth are all in COMM_NO_COMMUNICATION is a rule in BswM, not a decision inside EcuM. EcuM only does what the rule's action list calls.

4.4.0 and R20-11 have the same five phases and the same split between BswM and EcuM. In EcuM Fixed (the older EcuM variant with its own fixed state machine, 4.3.1 and older) EcuM went through its own states such as STARTUP, RUN, SHUTDOWN, SLEEP and WAKEUP; lessons 51 and 52 compare the two.

Things to notice. STARTUP and SHUTDOWN are short and mirror each other; almost everything an ECU does happens in UP, under BswM. One call, EcuM_GoDownHaltPoll, leaves UP; the shutdown target selected before decides whether that means sleep, reset or off.
