      Notifications

      Notifications are not configured in COM itself. They are configured by the user of COM, normally the RTE, in a container called ComUserCallback. The signal is linked to it with ComUserCallbackRef.

      What this tool does not know. It looks at one signal in one I-PDU. It cannot see the other signals in the I-PDU, the layers below COM, or the extra parameters your tool vendor adds. Handle numbers are left out because tools assign them. Names follow AUTOSAR release R25-11. In projects on older releases the notifications are configured on the signal itself, with names such as ComNotification and ComTimeoutNotification.
      

      


      Rules and names: AUTOSAR COM specification, R25-11, chapter 10. ARXML form: Specification of ECU Configuration, R25-11. More help: recipe book, troubleshooting.
