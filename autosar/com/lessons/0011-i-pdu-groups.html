AUTOSAR COM

I-PDU groups

An ECU should not always communicate. While the bus sleeps or during special modes, whole sets of I-PDUs are stopped, and started again later.

I-PDU groups

I-PDUs are sorted into named sets, for example "everything this ECU sends on bus 1" or "everything it receives on bus 1". Such a set is an I-PDU group (a set of I-PDUs that is started and stopped together). An I-PDU group holds only I-PDUs of one direction, transmission or reception, never both. Com_IpduGroupStart and Com_IpduGroupStop (the COM APIs that start or stop an I-PDU group) switch it as a whole.

After start-up

All I-PDU groups are stopped. Nothing is sent until an I-PDU group is started.

Who starts and stops?

Not COM, and not an SWC. In an AUTOSAR ECU it is the BswM (BSW Mode Manager: the module that decides on mode changes of the ECU). It knows the state of the bus: awake, going to sleep, diagnostics running.

An I-PDU may belong to several I-PDU groups. It is started as long as at least one of its I-PDU groups is started. An I-PDU that belongs to no I-PDU group is started at start-up and can never be stopped.

Try it

This I-PDU has the transmission mode MIXED with a period of 2 seconds, and Speed is a TRIGGERED signal. Its I-PDU group starts out stopped, as after start-up. Com_IpduGroupStart is called here without initialization, so stored values are kept (see below).

Things to notice. Write Speed while the I-PDU group is stopped, then start it: no I-PDU goes out at that moment, the old trigger is forgotten. But the first periodic transmission carries the value you wrote.

Starting with or without initialization

Com_IpduGroupStart has a parameter Initialize. With true, COM sets the data of the I-PDUs back to their init values, including the shadow buffers of their signal groups. With false, it keeps whatever is stored. In both cases the periodic transmission, the minimum delay time, deadline monitoring and the update-bits start from the beginning.

There is one more, smaller switch for reception: reception deadline monitoring alone can be disabled and enabled again, while the I-PDUs keep being received.
